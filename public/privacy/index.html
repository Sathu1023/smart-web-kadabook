Privacy Policy

Updated 13 November 2026 · Contact: matrixgroup.smart@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what data KadaBook (SMART (PVT) LTD) collects, how it is used and how it is protected.

1. What we collect

Account details: your name, phone number or email and shop name. Business data: sales, expenses, stock, credit, staff and branch records you enter. Shop verification (KYC) documents you upload. Device details: app version and basic error reports, used only to fix problems.

2. How we use it

To run the app: keep your data in sync across your phones, create reports and receipts, send the WhatsApp reminders you choose to send to your own customers, and help you when you contact us. We do not use your business data for advertising.

3. Where it is stored

Your data is stored securely with Google Firebase. KYC documents are kept in a private storage area that only KadaBook admins can open. If you connect Google Drive for backup, backup files are stored in your own Google Drive; KadaBook only accesses files it created (drive.file scope).

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that run the app for us (such as our cloud host), or when the law requires it.

5. Your choices

Export your data at any time from More → Data tools. Ask us to delete your account and data from More → Delete my account. Disconnect Google Drive backup anytime in the app; files already in your Drive stay under your control.

6. Security

Access to your data needs your login. You can also turn on a PIN lock so only you can open KadaBook on your phone.
